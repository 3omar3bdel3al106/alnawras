Who We Are

Engineering and craftsmanship under one roof

Alnawras was founded to give manufacturers a dependable local partner for stainless steel process equipment. Today our engineers, welders and installation teams deliver projects for food, beverage, pharmaceutical, chemical and industrial clients.

We handle every stage in-house — design, cutting, rolling, welding, polishing, testing and installation — which gives us full control over quality and delivery time.

  • Experienced engineering team
  • Certified TIG welders
  • Fully equipped workshop
  • Dedicated site teams
